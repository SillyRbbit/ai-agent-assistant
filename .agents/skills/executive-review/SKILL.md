# Executive review

1. Read `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, and the relevant verified report.
2. Separate implemented, mocked, planned, blocked, and prohibited capability.
3. Summarize purpose, user or operational value, controls preserved, evidence, residual risk, dependencies, and the decision requested.
4. Use plain business language while retaining material security and verification limits.
5. Identify the exact next milestone and acceptance gate.

Do not invent completion evidence, suppress material risk, change files, approve work, or present a mock as production capability.
