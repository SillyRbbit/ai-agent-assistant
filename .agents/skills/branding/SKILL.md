# Cortexa branding

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/branding/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` and the specific companion guide
   for the requested artifact.
2. Read D-026 in `DECISIONS.md` before changing human-facing names or
   compatibility identifiers.
3. Reconcile factual product claims with `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`,
   and `docs/product/ARCHITECTURE_BASELINE.md`.
4. Use only the canonical files under `assets/branding/`.
5. Preserve the full logo field, proportions, orientation, and colors. Do not
   trace, redraw, crop, extract, recolor, or remove its near-white field.
6. Keep repository, package, crate, executable, bundle, database, GitHub, IPC,
   and storage identifiers unchanged unless separately approved.
7. Do not replace `src-tauri/icons/` without a separately approved increment
   that generates and verifies the complete production icon family.
8. Verify every asset path, aspect ratio, supported background, alternative
   text choice, and rendered output before closeout.

For slides and architecture diagrams, follow
`docs/branding/PRESENTATION_GUIDELINES.md` and distinguish implemented, mocked,
proposed, and prohibited paths explicitly.
