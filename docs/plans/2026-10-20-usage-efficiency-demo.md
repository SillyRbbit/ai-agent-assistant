## Historical objective and boundary

The plan scoped a mock-only Activity panel with one no-input stateless Rust
projection getter, a closed TypeScript client and six fictional sources:
Simulation, OpenAI API, Anthropic API, LM Studio, Ollama and Codex. These labels
do not establish provider connectivity or live subscription access.

Rust owns bounded synthetic validation, deduplication, checked arithmetic and
fixed 7-/30-day projections. Counts are nonnegative JavaScript-safe integers.
Missing, Unavailable and Unverified remain distinct from supplied zero. Cached
input is a subset of input; reasoning is a subset of output; total is input plus
output. Cumulative records replace earlier versions rather than accumulating;
known numeric decreases are rejected across intervening absence. Identical
duplicate records are idempotent, conflicting duplicates reject. Daily/period
reconciliation preserves absence through empty buckets. Incomplete completed-task
coverage makes its average Missing. Failed/cancelled activity is not included in
completed-task averages. Account, task and daily totals are never added together.

Only approved numeric/categorical data and dates leave Rust. Synthetic internal
identifiers are stripped. The getter adds no file, database, clock, preference,
runtime, credential, network or process access. Mock labels, deliberate loading,
late-result rejection and empty/error/absence states are required.
